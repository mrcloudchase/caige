# cAIge Competency Matrix

**Version 2.0**

The competency matrix defines the cAIge role. For each of the program's 43 sections it lists the **knowledge** a certified engineer holds and the **skills** they can demonstrate. Every exam question maps to one section, and every section has a matching lab task.

This page is generated from the training content itself, so the matrix, the training sections, and the exam objectives cannot drift apart.

## What a cAIge holder delivers

A Certified AI Guardrail Engineer can take an AI system and produce:

- **A threat model:** the principals and their authority, the trust boundaries, the failure modes by category (safety, security, containment), likelihood and impact, and the residual risk to accept.
- **A guardrail architecture:** which controls go where and why, preferring deterministic and architectural controls wherever they are possible, and using probabilistic controls where they are not.
- **Working controls:** input and output handling, retrieval authorization, safety classification, structured output, least-privilege tools, sandboxing, budgets, action gating, an emergency brake, and tamper-proof audit.
- **Evidence:** evaluation and red-team results with defensible statistics, covering both harm and over-refusal.
- **Operational readiness:** monitoring, incident response, brake drills, re-validation on change, and release gates.
